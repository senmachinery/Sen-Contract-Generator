Sen Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Sen Publisher is an internal tool operated by SEN LABELS MACHINERY SDN BHD (Johor, Malaysia). It is used only by our own staff to publish our company's videos and posts to our own Facebook Page, Instagram account and YouTube channel.

What data we access

  • Basic information about our own Facebook Page and Instagram business account (name, ID, follower and post counts).
  • The content we choose to publish and the links to the resulting posts.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of other users.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.

Storage

Access credentials are stored only on our company computer and are never shared.

Data deletion

To remove the app's access, go to Facebook Settings > Business integrations and remove "Sen Publisher". To ask us to delete any data, email senlabelsinternational@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.
